Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

1. Overview

Flowcel is a local desktop application. All data processing happens entirely on your machine. We do not upload, transmit, or store your data files on any server.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is strictly necessary to operate the license system:

  • Email address — provided at purchase, used to deliver your license key and send renewal notices.
  • Machine identifier — a hashed, anonymous identifier of your device, used to enforce the single-machine license activation. It cannot identify you personally.
  • License key — stored locally on your machine and validated against our server on launch.

We do not collect: your data files, pipeline configurations, usage analytics, or any other personal information.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver and validate your license key.
  • To send subscription confirmation and renewal reminder emails.
  • To enforce single-machine activation.

We do not sell, share, or rent your data to any third party.

4. Data Storage

License data (email, machine ID, expiry date) is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, located in the United States. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your pipeline configurations and settings are stored locally on your machine in ~/.flowcel/ and never transmitted to us.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Polar.sh, which acts as the Merchant of Record. We never receive or store your payment card details. Polar.sh's privacy policy applies: polar.sh/legal/privacy.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your personal data (email and machine ID) at any time by contacting us. We will remove your data from our systems within 7 days.

7. Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify active subscribers by email of any material changes.
